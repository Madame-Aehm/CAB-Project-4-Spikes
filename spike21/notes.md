## SQL and Non SQL databases

- **SQL** refers to databases that use relationships between entries to reduce storage. Instead of duplicating data in multiple places, a link is established to the base location of some data. SQL databases were much more popular back when digital storage was more expensive. Now that developer wages far outweigh the price of storage, the priority is to make _using_ the databases simpler and faster, even if documents are much larger. 

- MongoDB is a **NoSQL** database, which means there isn't a direct relationship between documents.
